About the Application

Welcome to the Zimbabwe Infrastructure & GIS Web Application. This platform provides an interactive geospatial analytics interface designed to visualize key national infrastructure metrics across Zimbabwe.

Features integrated into this web application include:

  • Interactive visualization of regional provincial boundaries and district delineations.
  • Spatial distribution of health centres, secondary schools, and major urban centers.
  • High-resolution transport network analysis including national primary and secondary road networks.
  • Custom spatial navigation tools, search capabilities, and map extent resets for Harare.

Geospatial Data Sources

The layers rendered across this platform are sourced from open spatial repositories and institutional clearinghouses:

  • HDX Zimbabwe: Humanitarian Data Exchange (OCHA) – Administrative boundaries, operational health facility layers, and population datasets.
  • OpenStreetMap (OSM): OSM Contributors – Transport infrastructure, road classification networks, and populated places data.
  • ZIMSTAT & Spatial Repositories: National administrative datasets digitized via QGIS for spatial suitability analysis and geospatial infrastructure planning.
